I'm Camryn Peña, a Graphic Designer and Illustrator based in Houston, Tx

Since Childhood, I have always enjoyed expressing myself creatively through visual arts. However, into my teens and early adulthood I found a true passion for Media arts. Drawing and Painting has always been my preferred creative outlets but using my knowledge in Design Software like Adobe Creative Suite, I feel as though my work has the ability to reach its full potential with no limitations. Nothing excites me more than learning a new skill or taking on a new challenge. I hold the belief that life should be spent working on things that both inspire and challenge you, and it's this belief that has paved the way for me to work on this creative path within the design world. When I'm not glued to my computer, I like to spend my free time Crocheting, Drawing, Cooking, and Spending time with my family.
